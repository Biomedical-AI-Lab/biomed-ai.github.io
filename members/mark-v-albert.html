Mark V. Albert directs the Biomedical AI Lab. He is an Associate Professor of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of North Texas, with a joint appointment in Biomedical Engineering, and serves as Associate Chair for Graduate Studies.

His research uses machine learning to support clinical care, especially in rehabilitation and wearable sensing. Projects span Parkinson’s disease, stroke, transfemoral amputation and cerebral palsy, and current work includes video-based activity tracking, mobile robotics, and language and imaging models for health.
