Biomedical AI Lab

We use machine learning to advance medicine. Our roots are in wearable-device analytics that help clinicians treat mobility disorders, and our work now spans movement, sensing, language and imaging models for health.

Our research
Join the lab

Research themes

Movement & Gait Intelligence Scoring and classifying movement from instrumented gait, prosthetic and balance data. Wearable & Mobile Health Sensing Phones and wearables that detect falls and recognize activity and hand gestures. Language Models for Health & Humanitarian Text Language models on medical records, and on conflict and human-rights text. Medical Imaging & Multimodal AI Radiology report generation, visual question answering and vision-language models.

Populations we study

Parkinson's disease Incomplete spinal cord injury Transfemoral amputation Cerebral palsy Stroke Toddlers
