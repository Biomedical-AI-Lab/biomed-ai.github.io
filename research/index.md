# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-microscope" %}Research

We build machine learning methods that clinicians can use: models that score movement, detect falls, read clinical text and interpret medical images.
Our work is organized into four themes, and most projects cross more than one.

{% include themes.html %}

{% include section.html %}

## Movement & Gait Intelligence {#gait}

We turn instrumented gait, prosthetic and balance data into single, interpretable scores of movement quality.
This includes the Shriners Gait Index, an autoencoder-based summary of gait for children with cerebral palsy, validated across clinical sites; data-driven phenotyping of hemiplegic gait; and composite scores of prosthetic performance after lower-limb amputation.

## Wearable & Mobile Health Sensing {#wearables}

We use phones and wearable sensors to recognize activity and posture, detect falls and respond in real time, and measure how well everyday activities are performed.
Our work has covered Parkinson's disease, incomplete spinal cord injury, transfemoral amputation, stroke and toddlers, and now includes wearable hand-gesture recognition for people who cannot speak.

{% include list.html data="citations" component="citation" style="rich" filter="tags && tags.include?('wearables')" %}

{% include section.html %}

## Language Models for Health & Humanitarian Text {#language}

We fine-tune and probe language models for clinical records, such as pediatric fall risk and pain assessment, and for humanitarian text, such as human-rights annotation, conflict forecasting and peacebuilding reports.
We also study how to explain, distill and fairly evaluate these models.

{% include list.html data="citations" component="citation" style="rich" filter="tags && tags.include?('language')" %}

{% include section.html %}

## Medical Imaging & Multimodal AI {#imaging}

We build multi-agent and multimodal systems for radiology report generation and visual question answering, and survey where small vision-language models and multimodal language models are headed in medicine.

## Foundations
