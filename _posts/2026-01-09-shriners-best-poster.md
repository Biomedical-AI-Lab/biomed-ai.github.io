Our poster on SHAPE-UP, an AI approach to assessing arm function in children with arthrogryposis multiplex congenita, won the best poster award out of 19 posters at the 2026 Shriners Children's Annual State of the Science Meeting: AI and Orthopaedics in Atlanta.
Lab members also presented work on long-term outcomes after pediatric-onset spinal cord injury, fall-risk assessment with instruction-tuned language models, and attention-based updates to the Shriners Gait Index.
