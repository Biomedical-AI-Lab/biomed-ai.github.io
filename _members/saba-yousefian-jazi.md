Saba is a Computer Science and Engineering PhD student who has worked on markerless tumor tracking in lung imaging, emotion-based recommender systems, multispectral image analysis and point-cloud clustering. Her current project develops physiologically appropriate efficient-coding models of LGN/V1 spontaneous neural activity and their role in binocular vision, extended to audio, images and video.
