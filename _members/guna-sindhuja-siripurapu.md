Sindhuja joined the lab as a PhD student in Fall 2023. She works on Bayesian analysis to decode human preferences, combining computer science and neuroscience, and on estimating fall risk in older adults from postural sway with machine learning, presented as a poster at UNT's CCLS symposium in 2025. In Spring 2026 she was an external mentor, with Dr. Albert, for a senior capstone team building a gait-analysis clinical tool from video. Before joining, she was a research assistant in VR/AR/XR and game development.
