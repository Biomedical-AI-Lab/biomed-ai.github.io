Sindhuja's research combines computer science and neuroscience: she uses Bayesian analysis to model and decode human preferences. She also works on fall risk in older adults, estimating it from postural sway with machine learning (poster at UNT's CCLS symposium, 2025), and in Spring 2026 mentored, with Dr. Albert, a senior capstone team building a gait-analysis clinical tool from video. She joined the lab in Fall 2023, after working as a research assistant in VR/AR/XR and game development.
