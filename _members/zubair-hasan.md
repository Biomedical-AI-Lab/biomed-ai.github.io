Zubair's research is in AI for healthcare, with a focus on medical imaging and vision-language learning. He works on compact vision-language models, co-authoring a survey of small vision-language models in _Information Fusion_ (2025), and on medical image analysis: DFUSegNet, a boundary-aware attentive fusion network for diabetic foot ulcer segmentation (_Knowledge-Based Systems_, 2025), skin cancer detection from total-body photography (ISIC 2024) and pneumonia detection from chest X-rays. In the lab he also develops machine learning for recognizing toddlers' physical activity, and he manages the lab.

Before his PhD, he spent more than three years as a data scientist at Celloscope and Kona Software Labs and did research in the DSP Lab at BUET. He holds a BSc in Computer Science and Engineering from the Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST), and his teams were runners-up in the IEEE Video and Image Processing Cup 2020 and first on the live leaderboard of the Diabetic Foot Ulcer Challenge 2021.
