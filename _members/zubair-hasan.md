Zubair is a PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering, a research assistant working on AI for healthcare, and the lab manager. His research covers compact vision-language models, medical image segmentation and recognizing toddlers' physical activity with machine learning. He co-authored a survey of small vision-language models in _Information Fusion_ (2026) and DFUSegNet, a diabetic foot ulcer segmentation network, in _Knowledge-Based Systems_ (2025).

Before UNT, he spent more than three years as a data scientist in industry, at Celloscope and Kona Software Labs, and did research in the DSP Lab at BUET. He holds a BSc in Computer Science and Engineering from the Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST). His teams were runners-up in the IEEE Video and Image Processing Cup 2020 and placed first on the live leaderboard of the Diabetic Foot Ulcer Challenge 2021.
