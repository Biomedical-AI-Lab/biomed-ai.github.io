Olcay works on knowledge distillation and explainability for language models, including SAGE embeddings and LIME-LLM counterfactual probing, and has worked on zero-shot data augmentation and on detecting human-rights violations in social media during the Russia–Ukraine war. He has been a graduate research assistant at UNT since 2019 and was a teaching fellow in 2023. Before UNT he was at Columbus State University's TSYS School of Computer Science, where he co-authored a widely cited model for predicting online shoppers' purchasing intention in real time (_Neural Computing and Applications_, 2019).
