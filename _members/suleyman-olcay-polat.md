Suleyman's research is on making language models smaller and more transparent: knowledge distillation, including SAGE embeddings, and explainability through LIME-LLM, which probes models with fluent counterfactual edits. He has also worked on zero-shot data augmentation and on detecting human-rights violations in social media during the Russia–Ukraine war. Before UNT, at Columbus State University's TSYS School of Computer Science, he co-authored a widely cited model for predicting online shoppers' purchasing intention in real time (_Neural Computing and Applications_, 2019).
