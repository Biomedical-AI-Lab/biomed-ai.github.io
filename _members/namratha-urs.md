Namratha works across computational neuroscience and natural language processing. In the lab she showed how the early visual and auditory systems can be "derived" from an efficient coding of natural scenes and sounds, publishing a primer and accessible Jupyter notebook that derives neural receptive fields from grayscale, color and audio input (_Artificial Intelligence Review_, 2022) and presenting the work at the Society for Neuroscience meeting. Her NLP research covers discourse processing in UNT's Human Intelligence and Language Technologies Lab and explaining language models with counterfactuals (LIME-LLM).

She is also a software developer at Fermilab, where she works on GlideinWMS, distributed-computing software that runs physics workloads on grids, clouds and supercomputers, and she has spent a summer at Argonne National Laboratory.
