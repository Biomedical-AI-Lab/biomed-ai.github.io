Namratha pursues computational neuroscience: showing how the early visual and auditory systems can be "derived" from an efficient coding of natural scenes and sounds. She built a Jupyter notebook demonstrating this across grayscale, color and audio input, presented it at the Society for Neuroscience meeting, and spent a summer at Argonne National Laboratory.
