George works on natural language processing and trustworthy language models, including LIME-LLM, which explains model predictions with fluent counterfactual edits rather than broken text. He has more than five years of combined experience across NLP, computer vision, deep learning, reinforcement learning and MLOps.

He holds a bachelor's degree in electrical engineering from Romania and a master's in computer science from the United States. He spent three summers as a data scientist and machine learning engineer at State Farm, two years in the same role at UNT's High Performance Computing center, and has been a teaching assistant in computer science since 2020. He was a technical reviewer for Denis Rothman's _Transformers for Natural Language Processing_.
