Fahmida is a PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering who worked with Dr. Albert from Fall 2024 to Fall 2026 as a graduate research assistant, and taught as a teaching assistant for CSCE 1040 and CSCE 4115. Her research is in health informatics and clinical natural language processing: with Shriners Children's, she uses language models on pediatric electronic health records to estimate pain severity, predict the likelihood of surgery and assess fall risk in children with cerebral palsy (IEEE ICHI 2026).

Before her PhD, she spent three and a half years as a software engineer in Dhaka, at Dynamic Solution Innovators and Inovace Technologies.
