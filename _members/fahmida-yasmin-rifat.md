Fahmida joined the lab as a PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering in Fall 2024. Her research is in health informatics and clinical natural language processing: working with Shriners Children's, she adapts language models to pediatric electronic health records to estimate pain severity, predict the likelihood of surgery and assess fall risk in children with cerebral palsy. Her instruction fine-tuned model for pediatric fall-risk assessment, trained iteratively on generated clinical texts, appeared at IEEE ICHI 2026, and she has also worked on skin cancer detection from total-body photography (ISIC 2024).

Before her PhD, she spent three and a half years as a software engineer in Dhaka, at Dynamic Solution Innovators and Inovace Technologies.
