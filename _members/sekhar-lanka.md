Sekhar's PhD research focuses on artificial intelligence and autonomous agents: agentic and generative AI systems, multi-agent orchestration, retrieval-augmented generation and the evaluation and governance of enterprise AI. In April 2026 he co-led, with Dr. Albert, a hands-on UNT training on AI tools for teaching and scholarly work.
