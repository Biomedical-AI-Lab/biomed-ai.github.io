Himanshu joined UNT in Fall 2018, completed his MS in Computer Science in Spring 2020, and began the PhD program in Fall 2020. His dissertation develops a multimodal temporal fusion framework for automated echocardiogram report generation, building on earlier work compressing EKG signals with deep autoencoders. His interests include computational neuroscience and natural language processing.
