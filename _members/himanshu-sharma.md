Himanshu joined UNT in Fall 2018, completed his MS in Computer Science in Spring 2020, and began the PhD program in Fall 2020. His dissertation develops a multimodal temporal fusion framework, using cross-modal attention, for automated echocardiogram report generation, building on earlier work compressing EKG signals with deep autoencoders. He co-authored a 2023 _Sensors_ paper on a low-power wireless system that predicts sudden cardiac arrest on an embedded NVIDIA Jetson, and contributes to the lab's work on explaining language models with counterfactuals. His interests include computational neuroscience and natural language processing.
