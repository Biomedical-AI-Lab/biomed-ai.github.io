Himanshu builds multimodal deep learning for cardiology. His dissertation develops a multimodal temporal fusion framework, using cross-modal attention, that generates echocardiogram reports automatically, building on his earlier work compressing EKG signals with deep autoencoders. He co-authored a low-power wireless system that predicts early signs of sudden cardiac arrest with an optimized CNN on an NVIDIA Jetson (_Sensors_, 2023), and has contributed to LIME-LLM, the lab's work on explaining language models with counterfactuals, and to Census2vec, geo-embedded data for socioeconomic prediction. His interests also include computational neuroscience and natural language processing. He completed his MS in Computer Science at UNT in 2020 before starting the PhD.
